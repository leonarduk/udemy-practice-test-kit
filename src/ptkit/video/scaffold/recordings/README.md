One folder per lecture, one take per slide, named after the slide number:

    recordings/01-01-welcome/slide-001.m4a
    recordings/01-01-welcome/slide-002.m4a

`ptkit video script 01-01` prints what to read for each slide. Any of .wav,
.m4a, .mp3 or .flac works; prefer .m4a or .flac to keep the repo small.
Takes are loudness-normalised at build time, so don't fight your mic gain.
